Fees & M-Pesa

Fees that reconcile themselves.

Parents pay to your Paybill using the admission number. Elimuhub matches every payment to the right learner, issues the receipt and updates the balance — no more matching statements by hand.

  • Fee structures per class and term, billed in one click
  • M-Pesa Paybill and STK push, matched by admission number
  • Receipts, statements and balances always up to date
  • Bursaries, NG-CDF, capitation, expenses and budgets

FAQ

Questions schools ask us.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every school gets a 30-day free trial with all features. No card or payment needed to start.

Does it work for primary and secondary schools?

Yes. Elimuhub supports Pre-Primary, Primary, Junior School, Senior School and 8-4-4 secondary, including mixed and comprehensive schools.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Elimuhub runs in the browser. Parents and staff can also install it as an app from Google Play or straight from the browser.

What about parents without smartphones?

They still receive SMS messages from the school, such as fee reminders and notices, and keep paying fees through M-Pesa as usual.

Can we move our data from another system?

Yes. Import learners, guardians and opening balances from CSV. On Premium, our team handles the migration for you.

Who owns the data?

Your school does. Each school's records are kept separate, every user only sees what their role allows, and you can export your records at any time.

Who makes Elimuhub?

Elimuhub is a product of Pizza Technologies, a Kenyan software company.
